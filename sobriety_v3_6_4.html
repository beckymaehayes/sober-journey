What is your start date?

Set the day your count begins. This counter collects your progress toward a full year of sobriety, through every season. This is not a number that resets. It will count your sober days throughout the year even if they are not consecutive. Because bad days happen and they shouldn't negate the progress you've made.

Any day works. Your count begins here and moves forward through the year from this date.
